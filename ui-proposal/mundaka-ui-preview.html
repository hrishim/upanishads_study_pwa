तस्मै स होवाच । द्वे विद्ये वेदितव्ये इति ह स्म यद्ब्रह्मविदो वदन्ति परा चैवापरा च ॥ ४॥
tasmai sa hovāca . dve vidye veditavye iti ha sma yadbrahmavido vadanti parā caivāparā ca .. 4..
To him he said: Two kinds of knowledge must be known, as indeed the knowers of Brahman declare, the higher and the lower.

Word Meanings

Notes

The teaching distinguishes lower knowledge from the higher knowledge that points toward the imperishable.
